Slow Travel in Uttarakhand

Skip the rush. Spend more nights in fewer places and let the mountains set the pace.

Start now

What you need to know

1

Stay longer

Three to four nights in one base beats changing hotels every day and gives you real time to explore.

2

Go local

Choose homestays, eat at local kitchens and walk village trails to meet the region at its best.

3

Travel smart

Check weather and road status before every long drive. Monsoon months bring landslide risk.

🏔️

One loop, three moods

Rishikesh gives you the Ganga, cafés and yoga. Chopta and the Tungnath trail bring meadows and high-altitude views. Almora and Binsar offer quiet forests and old temple towns such as Jageshwar.

Together they make a week that feels full without feeling rushed.

A relaxed seven-day idea

1

Days 1 and 2: Rishikesh

River walks, the evening Ganga aarti and an easy first day.

2

Days 3 and 4: Chopta

Base yourself here and trek to Tungnath on a clear morning.

3

Days 5 to 7: Almora

Village walks, Binsar forest and the Jageshwar temples.

4

Keep buffers

Mountain drives are long and slow. Add spare time rather than packing every hour.

Common questions

When is the best time to go?

March to June and September to November are generally the most comfortable. Winters bring snow at higher altitudes.

Do I need to be very fit?

Not for most of this loop. Tungnath is a moderate trek, so go at your own pace and carry water.

How do I stay safe?

Check local weather and road updates, avoid night driving on mountain roads and share your plan with someone.
